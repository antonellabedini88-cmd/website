Independent robotics strategy & operations advisory

Robotics strategy, built to deploy.

I help robotics startups and scale-ups, VCs and private equity firms set the strategy and operating plan behind a robotics business: where to compete, what to build next, how to deploy it across use cases and sites, and whether it pays back.

  • Operational plans
  • Organisational structure
  • Strategy alignment
  • Prioritisation mechanisms

12 years in robotics, automation and industrialisation at Amazon Robotics, ABB Robotics, Tetra Pak and Philip Morris International, plus hands-on operating work inside private-equity portfolios.

Who I work with

Three seats at the same table

The investor, the buyer and the builder all need the same two answers: is the strategy right, and can the operation deliver it at scale? Each engagement is fixed in scope and ends with a decision-ready output.

Startups & scale-ups

Strategy & operating plan

"We need a first customer pilot that proves the business, and a plan investors will back."

  • Early stage: first-customer and pilot design, success criteria, fundraising readiness
  • Strategy and operating plan: segment focus, roadmap priorities, unit economics
  • Deployment playbook, validation gates and KPI framework
  • Field and applications team design, hiring bar
  • Go-to-market and sales support for first repeat customers
  • Fractional COO or Chief of Staff, board advisor
Output
Playbook your team runs without me
Timing
6–12 weeks, or monthly retainer
Venture capital

Strategic & deployment due diligence

"The demo is impressive. Is the market real, and will the model generalise to the next 20 use cases and sites?"

  • Market focus and go-to-market thesis: which segments will buy and why
  • Pilot evidence review: task accuracy and speed, interventions, uptime
  • Generalisation: how fast the model learns new use cases and sites
  • Deployment playbook and field-team maturity
  • Site-level unit economics and customer payback
  • Milestones to tie to the next tranche
Output
Readiness scorecard and red-flag memo
Timing
2–3 weeks, pre-term-sheet
Private equity

Automation strategy & value creation

"We're buying an integrator. Where is the margin hiding, and where is the risk?"

  • Investment thesis and buy-and-build strategy in automation
  • Operational diligence on integrators and automation platforms
  • Project delivery, cost overrun and backlog risk
  • 100-day plan and portfolio PMO set-up
  • Board-level reporting on deployment KPIs
Output
Diligence report and 100-day plan
Timing
3–6 weeks, then optional board role

What you get

A readiness scorecard, not a slide of opinions

Every diligence engagement scores the company on the evidence that predicts deployment at scale. Each rating links to the data behind it, and the verdict says what to fund, what to make a milestone, and what to walk away from.

How an engagement runs

Scoped up front, priced up front

  1. Diagnostic call

    30 minutes, free. You describe the company or deal; I tell you whether I can help and what I would look at first.

  2. Fixed-scope engagement

    A written scope, fee and deadline. Data-room review, site visits and interviews with the team and customers.

  3. Ongoing advisory

    Optional. A monthly retainer, a board seat, or a fractional operating role through the next funding milestone.
